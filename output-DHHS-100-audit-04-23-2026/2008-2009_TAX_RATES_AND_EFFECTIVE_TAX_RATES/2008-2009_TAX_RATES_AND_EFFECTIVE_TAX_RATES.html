(No special district rates are included in either county or municipal rates)

Effective rates are adjusted county and municipal rates which aid in comparison of jurisdictions. As counties are on various revaluation cycles, the ratio of assessed value to the actual value differ. These differences are adjusted by applying the sales assessment ratio (the ratio of selling price of property to the assessed value of property) to the tax rate to obtain an effective rate.
