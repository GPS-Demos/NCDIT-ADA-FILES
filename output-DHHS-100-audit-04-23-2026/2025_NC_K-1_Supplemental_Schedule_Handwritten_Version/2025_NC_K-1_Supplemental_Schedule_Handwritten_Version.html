Instructions for Handwritten Forms

Guidelines

[Image: Icon of an open book with a checklist showing a checkmark and an X]

Do not use red ink. Use blue or black ink.

[Image: Two document icons, one displaying a green checkmark and the other a red X]

Do not use dollar signs, commas, or other punction marks.

[Image: A rectangular box containing a dollar sign, comma, question mark, and curly brace, all crossed out with a red X indicating they should not be used]

Printing

[Image: Printer icon inside a dark gray circle]

Set page scaling to "none." The Auto-Rotate and Center checkbox should be unchecked.

[Image: Document icon with a downward pointing arrow indicating page scaling or alignment]

Do not select "print on both sides of paper."

[Image: Icon of a stack of printed papers]

Before You Send

[Image: Envelope icon displaying an @ symbol]

Do not submit photocopies of returns. Submit originals only.

[Image: Scanner or photocopier machine icon with a red X]

Do not mix form types

NC K-1 Supplemental Schedule 2025 Owner or Beneficiary's Share of N.C. Additions and Deductions

A pass-through entity, estate, or trust that reported N.C. additions or N.C. deductions to an owner or beneficiary on a NC K-1 form must provide each owner or beneficiary the information necessary for the owner or beneficiary to prepare the appropriate N.C. tax return. The pass-through entity, estate, or trust may use this schedule to provide the necessary information to the owner or beneficiary. (For more information, see the instructions for the appropriate N.C. tax return.)
