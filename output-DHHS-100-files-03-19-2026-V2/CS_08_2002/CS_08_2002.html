Dear County Director of Social Services:

Attn: Child Welfare Managers and Supervisors

Re: Child Welfare Training Schedules for March, April, and May 2002

As you are likely aware, we were unable to print a full training calendar for every DSS supervisor due to the budget crisis. Each agency was mailed one copy of the training calendar. In an effort to keep you updated on the training that will be offered, we will instead be providing you with the listing of our trainings on a monthly basis. Attached you will find all of the trainings that the Children's Services Statewide Training Partnership will be offering during the month of March, 2002 . If you are interested in viewing or down loading part or all of our training calendar this can be accessed by going to our website at:

http://childrensservices.dhhs.state.nc.us

. Our training calendar provides you with a description of every course, training requirements, information about and directions to our regional training centers and our registration form.

In order to register for one of the attached courses complete a registration form and fax it to the appropriate registrar:
