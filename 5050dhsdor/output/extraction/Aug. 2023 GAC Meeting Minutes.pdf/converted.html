Workforce Solution — Mose Dorsey

  • WIOA Equal Opportunity - 22 local areas that work with 81 NC Works Career Centers to serve the public. Career assessments, training & guidance, job fairs, workshops, and local job market information are some of the services provided. Assist with resume preparation, interviewing, and soft skills. Workforce Solution also have apprenticeships available. The Career Centers also focus on special populations such as veterans, ex-offenders, and individuals with disabilities for example.
  • DEI Compliance – Mose is also the ADA Compliance Officer for DWS. He makes sure all Centers are accessible and handles any grievances of discrimination. He works with new Division of Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion as well. EEO and DEI are very close so some benefits of ensuring diversity, equity, and inclusion would be the organization would grow faster, be more creative, and ensures everyone as access. Be mindful of the implicit bias we all have and micro-aggressions which can lead to discrimination. DWS offers training on DEI through free online courses.

GAC Happy Hour/Mentorship Program — Sam Westrich, Annette Lauber, Kathleen Wood

  • Once the group started getting into the details, they had to take a step back as it got very complicated. They have come up with a new idea called AT Happy Hour. Propose 1x a month the GAC will host and create a space for connecting individuals across the state that either use AT or are interested in using AT. The previous idea was more one-on-one and matching individuals to a mentor. This is a group activity instead where individuals could talk among themselves, share resources, talk about news or noteworthy topics in the field of AT. Each month could focus on a specific area of AT. The goal is helping people connect, so may need separate sessions with different timeslots.

AT Expo – October 5th, 2023 — Julie Dutchess, Christey Kirk

  • The AT Expo will be in-person for the first time since 2019. It will be at the McKimmon Center on NCSU campus in Raleigh. Starts at 9:00am, partnering with DPI who will be providing CEU’s for the event. It is free and open to the public, we will provide live stream of the event, and there will be vendors of AT devices and resources. The theme will be “A day in the life with Assistive Technology”. We are inviting anyone who uses AT or serves individuals with AT to tell their experience and present.
  • Don’t forget to register at https://ncsu.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_v5v0sCaTTJuymf0vT1dDtA and please distribute flyer. Right now, 194 total registrations with mostly professionals.
  • Begins at 9am and ends at 4pm, hope to have a keynote speaker with 45 minutes; next 2 sessions of 45 minutes each plus a 45 minute lunch. After lunch will be the Stephen E Sallee awards, then 3 more sessions and the closing remarks by Tammy Koger. The vendor hall will be open until 5pm.
  • Space for 30 vendors and 1 van. Right now, we have 12 vendors confirmed with payment. Trying to get a variety of AT categories. Still needs vendors for computer access, education, and recreation/leisure.
