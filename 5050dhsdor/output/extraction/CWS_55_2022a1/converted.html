Quarterly Regional CQI Meeting Agenda

10:00 – 10:20:

  • Welcome and Context:
    • Overview of CQI (name what the change is and how they are experiencing and building it with you)
    • State CQI Model
  • Discussion of data resources and their parameters and definitions

10:20 – 10:40: Preview of items for future meetings:

  • MFCV – timely documentation is key
  • Placement stability – understanding what qualifies as a placement change, coding correctly

10:40 – 11:15: Identify the Problem: Data Presentation

  • MOU measure data
  • Data on percentages of findings
  • Discuss the story behind the data

11:15 – 11:45: First Break Out - Explore root cause

11:45 – 12:15: Report out from Break Outs

12:30 – 1:15: Working Lunch (A PPT of announcements will be played during lunch and Division staff will be available for questions)

1:15 – 1:45: Decide on solutions: Second Break Out – Brainstorm and choose solutions to identified root causes

1:45 – 2:15: Report out on solutions

2:15 – 2:45: Implementation: Discuss barriers and how to break them

2:45 – 3:00: Wrap up
