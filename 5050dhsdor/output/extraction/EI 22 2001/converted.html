NOVEMBER/DECEMBER 2001

Dear County Director of Social Services:

Attention: Work First Supervisors and Program Administrators

Based on first of the month caseload data, we have noted an overall net increase in the Work First caseload in sixty (60) counties since August 1, 2001. From August to September, the overall Work First caseload increased by 1059 cases, the number of two-parent cases increased by 70, and the number of child only cases increased by 135. From September to October, the overall caseload increased by another 89 cases, and a net increase of nine two-parent cases and eight child only child only cases was noted.

It is certainly our hope that this increase will not develop into a trend, and we know that the net caseload size is affected not only by new approvals but also by the number of individuals leaving assistance. We also realize that counties are working with a Work First population that presents more challenges as they move toward self-sufficiency, therefore remaining on cash assistance for longer periods of time.

Based on our need to plan for potential impact on resources and service delivery should the caseload continue to increase, we need to obtain additional information regarding applicant characteristics and causal factors. We believe with better information about families being served, better decisions can be made regarding program priorities and policy issues. We anticipate that counties and state staff will experience an increase in requests for information about new applicants from a variety of sources, including social services boards, boards of county commissioners, and the media. We have discussed the recent caseload increase and need for additional information at several meetings of county DSS directors and staff within the past few weeks and have reached consensus that pertinent information is critical to overall program management.

Through the use of the attached data collection tool (an electronic version of the tool may be requested), we are requesting your assistance in obtaining characteristic and causal factor information on individuals for whom your staff completes applications for Work First or Benefit

The data collection tool has characteristic/causal factor information on the far left column; the remaining columns are for recording information on up to ten applicants. We are specifically asking that each worker record information obtained through the interview process when an application for Work First or Benefit Diversion is taken. It is suggested that an individual responsible for central collection of the data collection tool(s) for each worker be identified.

The completed documents should be mailed to Walt Johnson, Assistant Chief, Work First/Local Support by the 10th of December for the November 2001 information and the 10th of January 2002 for the December 2001 information.
