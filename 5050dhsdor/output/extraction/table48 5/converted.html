Freight car lines tax rate and base:

The property of freight line companies constitutes a special class of property. In lieu of all ad valorem taxes by either or both the State government and the local taxing jurisdictions, a tax of 3% is imposed on the total gross earnings received from all sources by such freight line companies within the State.
