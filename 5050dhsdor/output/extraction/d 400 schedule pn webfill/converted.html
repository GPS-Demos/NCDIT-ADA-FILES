Instructions for Web Fill-In Forms

Getting Started

  • Save the PDF to your computer
  • Use the latest version of Adobe Acrobat Reader to complete the form

Guidelines

  • Do not handwrite any information (TAX X)
  • Do not use commas when entering amounts (Enter Whole U.S. Dollars Only 99,999.00 X, 99999.00 Check)
  • Do not use brackets for negative numbers
  • Use a minus sign to show the amount is negative (Enter Whole U.S. Dollars Only [99999.00] X, -99999.00 Check)

Printing

  • Use the print icon on the form to ensure you have completed all required fields (PRINT)
  • Before printing, select "actual size"
  • Do not print on both sides of the paper

Before Mailing

  • Do not mix form types (NCDOR Web-Fill 11-17 X, NCDOR Web 11-17 IMPORTANT: Do not send a photocopy...)
  • Do not submit photocopies of returns
  • Submit originals only

A part-year resident or a nonresident who receives income from N.C. sources must complete this form to determine the percentage of total income from all sources that is subject to N.C. tax. You are a "part-year resident" if you moved to N.C. and became a resident during the tax year, or you moved out of N.C. and became a resident of another state during the tax year. You are a "nonresident" if you were not a resident of N.C. at any time during the tax year.
